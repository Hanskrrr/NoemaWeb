# Handoff 2026-11-13 (last): rooms

The founder wanted what he learns about one subject set together, whatever kind it is, instead of threads, sources and notes each repeating the others ("basically it is a mess"). He wanted it to work like a palace he builds outside his head. He judged the Obsidian graph uninformative and PARA a poor fit. We proposed rooms; he chose:
- their own sidebar section;
- right-click to put a thread, note or source in a room;
- choosing what is in a room from inside it;
- dragging things into rooms and into Sources.

The rest he left to us. Decisions are in [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-rooms.md); the fence exception is in AGENTS.md.

## What changed

- **Rooms, in the sidebar** (a house icon on the rail).
  - **+ New room** makes one. Each room shows how many things are in it.
  - **In no room yet** lists every source, thread and note not in any room, to drag from.
- **Putting things in.**
  - **Drag** any thread, source or note onto a room. From another list, hold it over the Rooms icon and the rooms show.
  - **Right-click** any of them (in the lists, in a room, in "In no room yet"). The menu ticks the rooms it is in, so you can put it in or take it out, and has **New room with it…**.
  - **Choose what is in it**, in the room: every source, thread and note, with a tick, and a find box.
- **What comes along.** A source brings the threads asked about it and their notes; a thread brings its notes; a side question goes in as its thread. It is worked out each time, so a thread asked tomorrow about a source in a room is in the room at once. What came along can be taken out, and stays out.
- **The room is a map.**
  - Things made from one another are one cluster: the source, its threads, the notes. Each card says what it is and when it last changed.
  - Each card also says what was done there: questions, side questions, and how many of them were left open. Its edge darkens where more was asked.
  - Lines join clusters only where something links them (split off from, merged into, a passage kept in view, a citation), and say which.
  - Noema places new clusters and keeps the places. You move them by dragging, or Alt+arrow keys; **Arrange** places them all again.
  - **Delete room** takes the room away, not what is in it.
- **Dropping from outside.** Files dropped on Sources (or its icon) are read as sources: PDFs page by page, Markdown, code and any text, refused in words if not text. So are links. Dropped on a room's map, they are put in the room too.
- **The Assistant** sees the rooms. It can list what is in each, and proposes **put_in_room**: an existing room by name, or a new one. It waits for Do it, with Undo.
- **Code.**
  - New: core `rooms.ts`; migration 019 (`room`, `room_item`); store methods and routes under `/api/notebooks/:id/rooms` and `/api/rooms/:id…`.
  - Web: `Rooms.tsx`, `RoomView.tsx`, `ContextMenu.tsx` (one right-click menu for the app), `drag.ts`; route `#/room/<id>`.
