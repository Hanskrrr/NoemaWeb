# Handoff 2026-11-06: sources (M3)

The founder said "go ahead" to M3 without saying what form his course material takes, so this follows the brief: pasted text and web pages first (arXiv papers from their HTML version), PDFs later. Decisions are in [ADR 0013](../adr/0013-sources.md).

## What changed

- **Add a source** from the sidebar (**Sources › + Add source**): a web page by its address, or pasted text. The page is read on this computer and kept as it was read. Any arXiv link (abstract, PDF or HTML) is read from arXiv's HTML version, or ar5iv's. A PDF is refused with a reason ("it is a PDF, which Noema cannot read yet. For a paper, give its arXiv link; or paste the text"). Text copied from a web page and pasted keeps its headings, lists, tables and math.
- **The reading pane.** The source scrolls by itself, with the conversation beside it. Select a passage, **Ask about this**, and the question opens on the right with the passage on top; the place in the text stays put. Follow-ups and side questions from the answer stay beside the text too. **← Back to reading** returns to the source with the passage scrolled into view and highlighted (`#/s/<source>/from/<thread>`); the browser's Back and Forward work.
- **Where you asked** (insight I1). Every passage asked about is underlined, with a mark in the margin that is darker the more was asked there (in the thread and its side questions), and its threads listed under it. With no thread open, the right side lists the passages, most questions first; click one to find it in the text.
- **Versions.** **Read again** fetches the page again: if the text changed, it becomes version 2, and what was asked stays with version 1. A thread opens the version it was asked about, marked "You are reading version 1 of 2", with **Read the latest**. Pasted text has one version.
- **In the sidebar**, a source (❡) lists the threads asked about it (›), each with its passage, and their side questions; it folds like a thread. **Contents** lists the headings; a page's own first heading is shown once, as its title.
- **Search** finds a source's title and any passage of its latest version; a hit opens the text at that passage with the words marked.
- **Delete** puts a source in the Trash, with Undo; the threads asked about it stay and say so.
- **What the model gets.** For a question about a passage: the section around it (heading to next heading, at most 6000 characters) in the system message, labelled as material to explain, not instructions, and the passage with the question. Side questions and split-offs keep it. The context manifest records it as a `source` part.
- **Server.** Migration 011: `source`, `source_version` (Markdown, hash, original HTML, extractor), `source_anchor`, `thread.source_anchor_id`. Routes: `GET/POST /api/notebooks/:id/sources`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/sources/:id`, `POST /api/sources/:id/refetch`, `POST /api/sources/:id/restore`, `POST /api/sources/:id/threads`. `Thread.sourceId`, `sourceVersionId`, `sourceQuote`; `ThreadDetail.sourceOrigin`. Search kinds `source` and `passage`.
- **Core.** `htmlToMarkdown` (no new dependency), `sourceBlocks`, `sourceHeadings`, `sourceSection`.
