# Handoff 2026-11-07: ask again, pins, export and import (M5 done)

The founder asked to build the rest of M5 and to discuss memory and the "new to you" idea later. This session built the three missing pieces. Decisions are in [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-ask-again.md), [0018](../adr/0018-export-and-import.md) and [0019](../adr/0019-pins.md).

## What changed

- **Ask again.** **↻ Ask again** under a finished answer (also how a failed, stopped or interrupted answer is retried) makes a new answer to the same question, with the same context, and makes it the one in use. The earlier ones are kept: an answer asked more than once has a **‹ 1 / 2 ›** switch, and reading an earlier one says so, with **Use this one**. The one in use is shown first, searched, and sent with new questions. Side questions asked from an earlier answer keep pointing at it, go on with it as their conversation, and Back from them shows it at the passage. A side question can also be asked from an earlier answer. **What was sent** shows the one on the screen.
- **Pins.** Select a passage and press **📌 Pin** (in a thread) or **📌 Pin to this thread** (in a source, with a thread open beside it). The passage is sent with every question in the thread and its side questions until unpinned, after the source in the system message; What was sent shows it as **Passages you pinned**. The thread lists its pins at the top, marks pinned passages in its answers (dotted underline), and a click shows the passage. At most 12 where a question is asked, each sent up to 2,000 characters.
- **Export and import.** **Export** in the header: **Download copy** (JSON: every table but the search index, version 2), **Download Markdown** (About you, notes with what their citations cite, threads with their side questions, sources; for reading), and **Import a copy**, into an empty Noema of the same schema version only, in one checked transaction; the app then reloads into the imported notebook.
- **Server.** Migration 013 (`pin`). Routes: `POST /api/messages/:id/regenerate`, `GET /api/messages/:id/revisions`, `POST /api/messages/:id/current`, `GET /api/messages/:id/context?revision=`, `POST /api/threads/:id/pins`, `DELETE /api/pins/:id`, `GET /api/notebooks/:id/export.md`, `POST /api/import` (up to 512 MB). `ThreadDetail` has `pins`; `CreateBranchRequest.anchor` takes an optional `revisionId`. The JSON export changed shape (version 2: `tables`); version 1 files cannot be imported.
- **Core.** Pinned passages in `assembleContext` and `describeSent`; `readable.ts` (`notebookMarkdown`, `demoteHeadings`); the export schemas.
- **Fake model.** Asked again, it adds "Asked again: this is answer N." so tests can tell answers apart.
- **e2e.** A third test server, empty, to import into (`EMPTY_PORT` 8797).
