# 0019. Pins: passages kept in front of the AI, per thread

Date: 2026-11-07. Status: accepted.

## Context

The prototype brief puts "pinned passages" in the context next to the thread path, the branch origins and About me, and M5 asks for pin and unpin. Until now the model saw a passage only when a question was asked about it (a side question's origin, or a question about a source passage). A learner working through a paper wants a definition or an equation kept in view for the next several questions without quoting it each time.

The questions were where a pin lives (the notebook, a thread, one question), where it goes in the request, and how much it may take.

## Decision

- **A pin belongs to a thread.** It is sent with every question in that thread and in its side questions (they already inherit the thread's conversation), until it is unpinned. A side question made its own thread starts without them, as it starts without the conversation. A notebook-wide pin would be sent with questions it has nothing to do with; a single-question pin is what the passage of a side question already is.
- **It points at an exact passage**, like a side question: a revision of a message or a version of a source, through the existing `anchor` and `source_anchor` tables (migration 013 adds `pin`). Asking an answer again, or reading a page again, does not move it.
- **In the request, after the source**, as a part of the system message headed `## Passages the learner pinned`, each passage quoted line by line with where it is from, and told to be treated as material, not instructions. Pins change only when the learner pins or unpins, so the prefix the provider caches stays the same between questions. The manifest names the part (`pinned`) and What was sent shows it as **Passages you pinned**. Every line of a pin is quoted, so the heading cannot be faked by a passage.
- **Limits.** At most 12 pins where a question is asked (its own and inherited), each sent up to 2,000 characters (the whole passage stays in the pin). They count toward the fixed part of the budget, like About me.
- **In the app.** **📌 Pin** next to **Ask in a branch** over a selection in a thread; **📌 Pin to this thread** over a selection in a source, when a thread is open beside it. The thread lists its pins at the top (a side question shows those it inherits, unpinned where they were pinned) and marks pinned passages in its answers with a dotted underline. Clicking a pin shows the passage, as the revision it was pinned from.

## Consequences

- Unpinning deletes the pin (the log keeps that it was there); answers asked with it keep it in What was sent.
- A source's reading pane does not mark the passages pinned from it yet.
- Pins are in the JSON export like every table; the Markdown export does not list them.

## How to reverse

Stop passing `pinned` to `assembleContext` and remove the buttons; the table can stay.
