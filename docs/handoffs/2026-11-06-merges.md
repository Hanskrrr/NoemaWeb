# Handoff 2026-11-06: merging threads and notes

The founder asked whether threads could be merged, the way git squashes: a side question folded back into the thread it came from, or two parallel threads brought together, perhaps by rewriting the child into where it diverged; and whether that would cause problems with the KV cache. We agreed to merge without rewriting ([ADR 0008](../adr/0008-merges-add-never-rewrite.md)) and to try it.

## What changed

- **Merge back** on a side question's origin card, and **Merge into…** on any thread (a picker of threads, except the thread itself and its side questions).
- **A draft first.** The AI writes what the thread found: conclusions, definitions, formulas, open questions, with `[E3]` citations into it. The learner can **Edit** it, **Use the whole exchange instead** (no AI; the exchanges as they are), **Try again**, **Cancel merge**, or **Merge**.
- **In the target**, a card "⤶ Merged from …" shows under the answer the side question came from (for a parallel thread, after the last message). Its citations open the source's exchanges. **Undo merge** removes it.
- **For the AI**, the merged text goes in front of the target's next question and stays with it, so what came before is the same prompt prefix as before (cache stays warm). Earlier answers are untouched. Labels are removed for the model.
- The merged thread is kept and marked ⤶ in the sidebar ("merged back" on the map).
- **Merge a note in** (on a note): pick another note, confirm; its blocks are added at the end, clashing citation labels are renumbered so each still opens its exchange, and the other note is removed.
- Migration 006 (`thread_merge`). Server: `POST /api/threads/:id/merges`, `GET|PATCH /api/merges/:id`, `POST /api/merges/:id/{redraft,accept,undo,discard}`, `POST /api/notes/:id/merge`. Drafts cut off by a restart are marked failed.
