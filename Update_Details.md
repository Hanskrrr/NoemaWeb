# Update Details

## 中文

以之前的 `notebook-features` 为基准，当前版本主要做了这 7 项改动：

1. **Room 新增 Overview 首页。** 可以设置学习目标、从主题入口跳到 Board，也可以直接接着上次未完成的追问。没有未完成的追问时，会打开房间里最近的对话。
2. **新建空 Room 时突出材料入口。** 页面上方会临时显示醒目的 **Choose what is in it**。使用入口、跳过或离开房间后，按钮回到原来的位置。
3. **Board 默认按主题分区。** 已归到主题的材料会显示在对应区域，其余放在 **Ungrouped**。想手动摆放时，可以切到 **Free layout**。
4. **Board 连线可以聚焦查看。** 点卡片上的 **◎**，就能只看这张卡的关联线。需要查看全局时，点 **Show all links**。
5. **不同类型的材料卡可以互相包围。** 把对话、笔记或资料卡拖到另一张卡上，松手后确认 **Place inside**。父卡可以展开和折叠，子卡也可以移出。包围只影响这张 Board 的布局，原有材料和连线关系会保留。
6. **移动、连线、缩放有各自的控件。** 左上角把手用于移动，卡边圆点用于连线，右下角用于调整主卡大小。尺寸有上下限，刷新后可以恢复。
7. **Concepts 长页面支持拖拽滚动。** 从底部拖住一份未归类材料，把鼠标停在可见区域的上缘，页面会自动向上滚动，直到你能把材料拖进顶部的概念卡。

## English

This version includes seven changes from the previous `notebook-features` baseline.

1. **Rooms have a new Overview page.** Set a learning goal, jump to the Board from a topic, or resume an unfinished side question. If there is no unfinished side question, the resume action opens the room's most recent conversation.
2. **New empty rooms highlight where to add materials.** The **Choose what is in it** button is temporarily shown prominently at the top of the room. It returns to its usual location after you use the picker, skip the prompt, or leave the room.
3. **The Board groups materials by topic by default.** Materials assigned to a topic appear in its section, with the rest under **Ungrouped**. Switch to **Free layout** to arrange cards manually.
4. **Focus on a card's links.** Click **◎** on a card to see only its connections. Click **Show all links** for the full view.
5. **Nest cards of different material types.** Drag a conversation, note, or source card onto another card, release, and confirm **Place inside**. Parent cards can be expanded or collapsed, and child cards can be moved back out. Nesting affects the layout of that Board and preserves the original materials and links.
6. **Moving, connecting, and resizing have separate controls.** Use the top-left handle to move a card, the dot on its edge to draw a link, and the bottom-right handle to resize the main card. Sizes have upper and lower limits and are restored after a refresh.
7. **Long Concepts pages scroll while you drag.** Drag an unclassified material from the bottom and hold the pointer near the top of the visible area. The page scrolls up automatically so you can drop the material onto a concept card near the top.
